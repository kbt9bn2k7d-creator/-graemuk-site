About GRAEM UK

Construction experience with a hands-on approach.

GRAEM UK Ltd is a construction and refurbishment business built around practical trade experience, property supervision and multi-trade coordination.

Our initial focus is on voids, repairs, maintenance and refurbishment packages that can be planned properly, communicated clearly and delivered to a professional standard.

Specialist electrical, gas and other regulated works are coordinated through appropriately competent trades where required.
