About GRAEM UK

Practical Experience. Carefully Managed Work.

GRAEM UK is a construction, refurbishment and property maintenance company serving London and the South East.

GRAEM UK brings practical building experience and complete project management together, covering extensions, conversions, refurbishments and property maintenance.

Our experience spans running projects from structural works through to finished interiors, coordinating trades, managing the day-to-day work and maintaining attention to quality throughout.

We understand what matters to our clients: an agreed scope, realistic timescales, clear communication and work completed with care. Our approach combines practical involvement with organised management, keeping clients informed as their project progresses.

Whether improving a family home, renovating an investment property or preparing a property for its next tenancy, we aim to make the process straightforward from initial enquiry through to handover.
